What this compares

One look answers from one full image. Look again can request the full image again. Choose a crop can select a region or revisit the full image. The two agents have up to five views, including the first, and may answer early.

Crops use original-image coordinates and resize to 1,024 pixels on the longest side. All inputs use high image detail. Answers and dataset metadata are kept outside the agent's inputs. The replay shows recorded views and observable tool actions; it does not reconstruct hidden reasoning.
